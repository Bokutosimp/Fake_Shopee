# fake_shopee

ShopStack promised the best mechanical keyboards at prices too good to be real —
and, well, the site is about as real as the discounts. You landed on it chasing a
deal, but the deeper you click, the more the shop feels held together with tape.

A storefront out front, a locked back office somewhere behind it, and a staff that
clearly never expected a curious customer to start poking at the seams. So poke.
Sign up, look around, and see what this little shop remembers about you — and what
it's hiding in the back.
